To Pickle Salmon
The NewCastle way
Take a Salmon about 12 Pounds weight, Gut it
and take off the Head, cut it a cross into pieces, but
dont split it, scrape the blood clean from the bone
and wash it clean, then tye the pieces across each
way, set on your Fish pan with 2 quarts of Water
and three guarts of Strong Beer, half a pound of Bey Salt
& one pound of common Salt, when it boils scum it
well, then put in as much Fish as your liquor will
cover, and when it is enough, lay the Fish carefully
on Earthen Dishes, and let it stand tile next Day,
then put it by in pots, and add to the Liquor,
Three Quarts of Strong Beer Allegar; half an ounce of
Mace, the same of Cloves, and black pepper, one Ounce
of long pepper, 2 ounces of white Gingers sliced, boil
Them well together, half an hour, then pour this
Pickle boiling hot upon your fish, when cold cover
it close with strong brown paper,
This will keep a year round
Somerton
