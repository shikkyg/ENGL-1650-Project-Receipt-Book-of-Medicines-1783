For a Leprosy or Scurvy
One ounce of Quick Siler to a quart of
Water boiled in a new pipkin then put
into a botte, and dab the face & other parts
affected
Take a teaspoon ful once a day
for about three week, then stop
a little from taking it inwardly
The quick Silver will do for years
boiled in fresh water,
