an Electuary for a pheumatie Head-ach
Guiacum powder, a quarter of an ounce.
Cream of Tarter, a quarter of an ounce,
Crabs eye powder, half an ounce,
Mix these well togethen on paper, then beat
some of the powder so mixed well in Treacle
to a smooth Electuary,
Take a tea spoonful
every morning or night ten days then
rest and take again

Mr Burk
