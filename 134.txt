134 a cure for a Cancer
given me by Mr Merideth
1805
Take the newest & best Turkey figgs you can get
Boil them in Milk, which will thicken in
boiling, when quite done. Split the figs, and
apply them as warm as you can bear them to the
Cancer wither it is broke or not; it must be washed
with the Milk warm several times in the day, the
figgs to be applied fresh morning & Evening binding
them gently on for some time and drinking a quarter
of a pint of the Milk the figs are boil'd in Morning
& evening, the quantity of figs boiled each time must
be proportioned to the size of the place they are to cover,
this method must be strictly observed for four Months
the first application will be attended with pain, but
the patient will find ease in every Poultice,
The cancer of an old man who died at the age of 105
was cured by the above some years before his death, the
Cancer had began in one corner of his mouth it had
eat thro his jaw & cheek, & half way down his throat
yet he was so perfectly cured that he never found the
least return, And a woman who had a cancer in
her Breast ten years, which used to bleed so much that
it was judged more of an Ulcer than a Cancer she was
cured by the above after the use of 12 pounds of Figs,
