A Seed Cake
One Pound of Butter, washd in Rose Water,
One Pound of Sugar, One Pound of Flower, 12 new
laid Eggs beat & straind, 2 Ounces of seeds, strewd
in by degrees,
Bake this Cake One Hour & a half,
Mr Trevilla
