To make Cucumber Vinegar
Take a Quart of the best white wine Vinegar,
pare & slice 15 large cucumbers, let it steep
three or four days, add 4 or 5 onions, Sliced
a few shallots, a little Garlick and some ground
pepper, a little Cayan pepper and some Salt,
Let these boil up and when cold strain it off
and bottle it for use

To Pickle Salmon
Wash your Salmon Slit it in two, lay it in
An Earthen dish or Pot, one peice over the other
betwixt over under, Strew Jamaica & whole
black pepper, Bay leaves & Salt, put in as much
Vinegar & water as will cover it, tie a double
paper over the Pot & bake it
It is good to eat hot or cold
