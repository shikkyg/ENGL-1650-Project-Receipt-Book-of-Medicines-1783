To Make Current Wine
To every quart of Juice, put 3 quarts of spring water
and to every Gallon of Liquor, 4 pounds of Lump
Sugan, stir it well up, and let it remain in the
Tub until done working, and the sugar disolved
then put it into your Barrel, and add one quart
of Brandy to every 14 Gallons, do not stop it down
until it has done fermenting, but put a light
Covering over the Bunghole,
It must not be shun'd but well stird up
Mr. Daniel Giberne
