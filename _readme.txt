The information in this recipe collection are annotated into three categories: Table of Contents, Medical Recipes, and Culinary Recipes. Of the latter two sections that contain recipes, in order to improve ease of recipe searching, the year (if provided) and contributor(s) (if provided) are indicated. Each page is also provided a unique metadata identifier as the 25 pages were selected from a larger pool of 186.



The Transkribus HTR worked surprisingly well. I've developed a level of literacy in reading this handwriting style and there are a few nunances that I noticed to build a mental defition of the script. I was surprised to see these intricacies easy dealt with by the AI, the training data must've looked similar to my text. I did noticed however, that the AI failed accurate detection in areas that were subject to the writer's preference. For example, the swirls and accents that one might decide to add to a capital letter or beginning of page all vary according to who was writing. Given that this text was owned and edited by one family, it's clear that multiple authors contributed to the text. While the text had no tables or complex graphics to work around, I did notice that the AI struggled slightly with the table of contents structure. It didn't detect the page number and the page title as the same line. I was forecd to manually rewrite those pages since the page number did not correctly align in the transcription with what it was representing.
