To Make Mead
Mr Pearse
Ten quarts of Water to one quart of Honey, put
the honey in when the water is warm, 3 quarters of ap
of the finest Loaf Sugar, let it boil one hour, skim
it well, and then put in two or three races of Ginger and
ten Cloves, and when taken off the fire, put in the juice
of 2 Lemons with the rines, and when cold put into it
a toast of bread spread with 3 or 4 spoonfuls of new Ale
yeast, and while the toast is hot put it in the Vessell
Stop it very close & let it work for a week then bottle it
It will soon be fit to drink
To Make Mead
To 10 Gallons of Water, put two Gallons of Honey
a handful of Race Gingers & two Lemons cut
in peices, put these in and boil it very well
keep it skimming, and let it stand all night
in the same vessel it is boil'd, the next
Morning Barrel it up with two or three spoonfuls
of yeast
Bottle it in 3 weeks or a month
