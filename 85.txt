Boil
In Aleppo, (Syria) the natives as well as
foreigners of both sexes are attacked with a
kind of Boil which breaks out in Various
parts of the body, which suppurates at the end
of the year and heals leaving a scar, this
is attributed to the subtility of the air of
Aleppo, or by drinking unboiled water
this Malady is announced by a fever, and
the method of cure is very simple,
Nothing more is necessary than to lay on
an Ivy leaf with a little cerate spread upon
the leaf, over the tumour- and this brings it
to a suppuration in the course of a year.
no particular regimen is required; and
when a cure is effected the body generally
enjoys good health for a long time after
Those people, who during their residence at
Aleppo haveing never drank water till it had
been boiled remained free from this distemper
