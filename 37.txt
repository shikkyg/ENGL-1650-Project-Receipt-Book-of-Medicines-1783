Pills for a Bilious habit, prescribed by a
Physician at Nante who says, Allors thees
prepared will not disagree with the most tender
Bowells,
Alors, three quarters of an ounce
Mastick, one quarter of an ounce
powder them seperately, & incorporate them
with a sufficient quantity of syrup of wormwood
to make a Map for Pills

Great care must be taken to prepare
the Alors properly with juece of Violelts or with
a very strong tincture of Liquorice equally good,
& the latter is generally used being always to be
had,
One or two Pills is a Dose immediately
before Dinner or Supper,
Mr Parsons Wore
