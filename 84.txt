Gravel
1811 From Bath Herald April
A philanthropist begs to communicate to the world
the following simple remedy for the Gravel:
Desolve three drachms of prepared nitre in a
quart of cold water, and take half of this quantity
in the course of the day. Continue this medicine
for a few days and that painful complaint will
be removed. it may be taken at any hour.
but is best after a Meal
Opening Medecine
1/2 a pound Treacle
1 ounce Milk of Sulpher
1/2 an ounce of Cream a Tarter, or Magnisha
Mix
& take a tea spoonful over Night
