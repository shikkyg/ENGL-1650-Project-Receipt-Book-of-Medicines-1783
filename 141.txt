To Make Mead
To
4 pounds of honey put one Gallon of Water
and the whites of three or four Eggs, then Boil it
till the scum rises, and scum it till 'tis fine
Strain it off and work it, luke warm, with a
very little yeast, and when it begins to work
put in a handful of dry Hops & sun it in
about 3 days, leave the Barrel unstop'd for
three or four days or untill it is done working

When Oranges are in season put the Juice
of one Orange to every Gallon of Wine, with
three parts of the peel cut very thin,
