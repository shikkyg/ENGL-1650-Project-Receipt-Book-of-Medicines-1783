To Make Orange Posset
Squeeze the Juice of four Sevile Oranges, into
a China Bason that holds about a quart, Sweeten it
with Lump Sugar like a syrup, put in 4 spoonfuls
of Orange flower water, & strain it thru a fine seive,
Boil a pint & half of thick cream, with a little of the
Orange Peel in it cut very thin, and when it is cool
not too cool pour it into the Bason of Juice, thro'
a funnel, held as high as you can from the Bason,
it shoud stand a Day before you use it,
Miss Dewbery
