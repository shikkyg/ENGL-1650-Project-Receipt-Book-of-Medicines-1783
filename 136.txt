Milk Punch
To 5 Quarts of Brandy put the peel of 8 Oranges, &
of 8 Lemons, pared very thin, let them infuse
12 Hours, have ready boild & cold again 8 quarts
of water, with 4 pounds of double refin'd sugar,
Mix the water & the Brandy together, adding the
Juice of the 8 Oranges and 6 Lemons, then
strain it from the Peels, and barrel it up close,
Let it stand Six Weeks, then Bottle it,
When you put it into the Barrel
You may add 3 pints of warm Milk
from the Cow, or omit it just as
You please
Syrup of Oranges
To every Winchester quart of Juice, put 2 pd & 1/4 of
Single refind Sugar, keep it in an Earthen pan,
3 weeks or a Month, & Stir it well every Day, put
a guart of Rum or Brandy, to every Gallon of Juice
then bottle it without seimming and keep it in
a cool Dry place,
Mr Trev.
