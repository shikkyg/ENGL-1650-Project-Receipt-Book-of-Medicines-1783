Tooth Ach
82
Take a clean tobacco pipe put the Bowl into
the fire till red hot, then put in it a pinch or
two of hin bane seed, and put a little funnel
over the bowl & let the vapour from the narrow
pipe of the funnel be applied to the aching tooth,
and when the pipe gets cold, repeat the above
for a quarter of an hour, wrap warm & retire
soon to bed.
Cured the Prince of Wales
Bath Herrald Jan 4 1810
Sir Stephen Gox
