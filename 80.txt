a cure for a Cancer
80
Take the newest & best Turkey Figgs you can get,
boil them in milk which will thicken in boiling,
When quite done split the figgs and apply them as warm
as you can bear them to the Cancer, whether it is
broke or not, it must be wash'd with the Milk warm
several times in the day.
The figgs to be applied
fresh Morning &Evening. binding them gently on
for some time and drinking a quarter of a pint
of the milk the figgs are boiled in morn g & even g.
The quantity of figgs boiled each time must be
proportioned to the size of the place they are to
cover, this method must be strictly observed for
four months
The first application will be attended
with pain - but the Patient will find ease in every
Pouthice
in his Jaw
The Cancer of an old man, who died at the Age of 105
was cured by the above some years before his Death.
and a Woman who had a cancer in her Breast Ten
years. It used to bleed so much that it was judged more
of an Ulcer than a cancer but by repeating this Preep
She was cured by the use of 12 pounds of Figgs.
